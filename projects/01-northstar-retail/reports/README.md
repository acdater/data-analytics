# Findings

Sales increased by 8.14% in 2025, but COGS grew by 14.69%. Gross profit fell by 5.19%, and gross margin dropped from 32.95% to 28.89%.

My recommendation: investigate costs and selling prices for the same products, review discount effectiveness and focus on category-level margin improvement.
