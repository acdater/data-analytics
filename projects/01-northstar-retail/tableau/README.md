# Tableau Dashboard

I built a profitability dashboard for 2024-2025 with four KPI cards, YoY changes, monthly sales and margin trends, and category rankings by net sales and gross profit.

View the [interactive dashboard on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/alexei3256/viz/NorthstarRetailProfitabilityAnalysis20242025/NorthstarProfitabilityOverview), or open the [packaged workbook](01-northstart-retail-dashboard.twbx) in Tableau Desktop. Start with the KPI cards, then compare monthly trends and category performance. The KPI cards and category rankings show 2025, with 2024 as the YoY baseline.

The reporting CSVs are in [data/processed](../data/processed/). If a source needs relinking, use these files. Results follow the notebook's delivered-order scenario, including refunds and assumed 80% inventory recovery.
